Director Kevin Connor offers a wealth of technical details and production stories in this commentary moderated by Bill Olsen, but Olsen--who opens declaring himself a last-minute replacement--can't get out of his own way. 

Over the opening credits, Olsen confidently asserts that the steelworks footage is stock. You can almost feel Connor's pain as he, ever the polite Englishman, corrects him: they shot it for the picture.

This pattern continues throughout. Even worse, Olsen takes detours to tell personal anecdotes about meeting this or that producer or director. The questions he does ask Connor are rarely open-ended. Most invite a simple yes or no answer. It's hard to tell if Olsen was nervous or just oblivious to Connor's discomfort, but the whole enterprise is painfully awkward.

That said, viewers who detest scripted, academic commentaries may find this a refreshing change. Consider yourselves warned.
